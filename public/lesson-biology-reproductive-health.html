Reproductive Health

Reproductive Health

Key concepts

  • Reproductive health includes physical, emotional and social well-being related to reproduction. Accurate education and accessible care are central.
  • Contraceptive methods differ in mechanism. Barrier methods can also reduce transmission of many sexually transmitted infections.
  • In vitro fertilisation involves fertilisation outside the body followed by embryo transfer. Assisted methods are selected by qualified clinicians.

Connect the ideas

A condom acts as a physical barrier; a contraceptive pill works through hormonal mechanisms. They are not interchangeable in all effects.

Common mistake

These notes describe exam concepts, not individual treatment advice. No contraceptive method guarantees prevention of every infection.

Practice questions and explanations

Try each question before opening its answer.

1. Which method is a barrier contraceptive?

  1. Condom
  2. IVF
  3. Embryo transfer
  4. Ovulation
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Condom

Condoms create a physical barrier.

2. In IVF, fertilisation occurs:

  1. Outside the body
  2. Only in the uterus
  3. Inside the urinary bladder
  4. In the cervix only
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Outside the body

Gametes are brought together outside the body under laboratory conditions.

3. Which statement about contraceptive methods is correct?

  1. All prevent every STI
  2. They differ in mechanism
  3. IVF is contraception
  4. All are surgical
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. They differ in mechanism

Barrier, hormonal, intrauterine and surgical methods use different mechanisms.

4. Vasectomy involves interruption of the:

  1. Vas deferens
  2. Ureter
  3. Urethra necessarily
  4. Ovarian follicle
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Vas deferens

It blocks sperm transport; it does not involve removing the testes.

5. Tubectomy involves interruption of the:

  1. Oviducts
  2. Urinary bladder
  3. Ureters
  4. Cervix only
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Oviducts

It prevents the normal meeting of gametes through the oviductal pathway.

6. An intrauterine device is placed in the:

  1. Uterus
  2. Kidney
  3. Testis
  4. Lung
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Uterus

Different IUD types use different contraceptive mechanisms; insertion and care require appropriate clinical services.

7. A sexually transmitted infection can be prevented or detected more effectively with:

  1. Education, suitable prevention and testing
  2. Reliance on absence of symptoms only
  3. Assuming every method prevents every infection
  4. Antibiotics without diagnosis always
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Education, suitable prevention and testing

Some infections can be asymptomatic; education and appropriate testing are important.

8. ICSI involves introduction of a sperm directly into an:

  1. Oocyte
  2. Endometrium
  3. Ureter
  4. Blood cell
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Oocyte

Intracytoplasmic sperm injection is an assisted fertilisation technique performed in a laboratory.

9. Reproductive health includes:

  1. Physical, emotional and social well-being
  2. Only absence of pregnancy
  3. Only fertility
  4. Only surgery
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Physical, emotional and social well-being

It is broader than a single organ function or reproductive outcome.

10. Infertility evaluation appropriately considers:

  1. Both partners and relevant biological factors
  2. Only one partner automatically
  3. Only body weight
  4. Only blood group
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Both partners and relevant biological factors

Causes can involve either partner or multiple interacting factors, so evaluation should not assign blame without evidence.

11. Reproductive health encompasses physical, emotional and social aspects of:

  1. Only puberty height
  2. Reproduction
  3. Only fertility counts
  4. Only contraception sales
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Reproduction

The concept is broader than absence of reproductive disease.

12. Accurate reproductive education helps reduce:

  1. Misconceptions and misinformation
  2. All genetic variation
  3. All hormone production
  4. Every population interaction
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Misconceptions and misinformation

Knowledge supports informed understanding and decisions.

13. Contraceptive methods primarily aim to prevent:

  1. Every infection automatically
  2. Every hormonal change
  3. All fertilisation research
  4. Pregnancy
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Pregnancy

Different methods interrupt reproductive processes through different mechanisms.

14. A barrier contraceptive acts mainly by preventing:

  1. All embryo chromosome segregation
  2. All menstrual cycles
  3. Sperm reaching the fertilisation pathway
  4. All ovarian hormone production
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Sperm reaching the fertilisation pathway

A physical barrier blocks sperm passage under appropriate use.

15. Condoms are classified as:

  1. Ovulation-inducing drugs
  2. Barrier methods
  3. Surgical methods
  4. Intrauterine devices
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Barrier methods

Their physical action separates sperm from the reproductive tract.

16. A diaphragm is placed to cover the:

  1. Cervix
  2. Ovary
  3. Kidney
  4. Seminiferous tubule
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Cervix

It provides a cervical barrier when correctly used.

17. A spermicide acts mainly on:

  1. Only ovarian chromosomes
  2. Only liver enzymes
  3. Only fetal bones
  4. Sperm viability or function
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Sperm viability or function

Its contraceptive role involves reducing the ability of sperm to participate in fertilisation.

18. Copper-containing IUDs influence sperm activity through:

  1. Only bone formation
  2. Only placental growth
  3. Copper-related effects in the uterine environment
  4. Only thyroid stimulation
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Copper-related effects in the uterine environment

The local action can reduce sperm motility and fertilising capacity.

19. Hormonal contraceptive action can include suppression of:

  1. All red-cell production
  2. Ovulation
  3. All kidney filtration
  4. All protein synthesis
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Ovulation

Feedback effects can prevent the normal ovulatory sequence.

20. Vasectomy interrupts the:

  1. Vasa deferentia
  2. Ureters
  3. Urethra alone
  4. Seminiferous tubules by removing the whole testes
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Vasa deferentia

Blocking these ducts prevents sperm entering the ejaculate pathway.

21. Tubal sterilisation interrupts the:

  1. Ureters
  2. Vagina alone
  3. Ovarian blood supply necessarily
  4. Uterine tubes
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Uterine tubes

Tubal interruption prevents ordinary gamete transport and meeting.

22. A vasectomy does not inherently require removal of the:

  1. Sperm transport route
  2. Duct lumen
  3. Testes
  4. Blocked duct segment
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Testes

The operation targets the sperm-conducting ducts rather than the gonads.

23. In vitro fertilisation means fertilisation occurs:

  1. Only without gametes
  2. Outside the body in a laboratory system
  3. Only inside the uterus
  4. Only in a testis
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Outside the body in a laboratory system

The resulting embryo may then be transferred through an appropriate assisted procedure.

24. ICSI involves injection of a sperm into an:

  1. Oocyte
  2. Endometrial cell
  3. Red blood cell
  4. Sertoli cell
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Oocyte

Intracytoplasmic sperm injection assists gamete fertilisation.

25. Artificial insemination introduces prepared semen into a suitable:

  1. Kidney
  2. Brain ventricle
  3. Bone marrow
  4. Female reproductive location
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Female reproductive location

It assists sperm delivery without normal intercourse.

26. The term infertility generally concerns difficulty achieving:

  1. Every type of immunity
  2. All hormonal activity
  3. Pregnancy
  4. Puberty
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Pregnancy

Causes can involve either partner or multiple factors.

27. In assisted reproduction, embryo transfer places an embryo in a suitable:

  1. Blood capillary
  2. Reproductive site
  3. Urinary bladder
  4. Testicular duct
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Reproductive site

Transfer supports further development in an appropriate location.

28. A sexually transmitted infection can spread through:

  1. Sexual contact
  2. Only shared textbooks
  3. Only visual observation
  4. Only chromosome counting
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Sexual contact

Transmission route is part of the STI classification.

29. HIV infection is not transmitted simply by:

  1. Exposure to infected blood
  2. Shared contaminated injection equipment
  3. Unprotected sexual exposure
  4. Ordinary handshaking
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Ordinary handshaking

Casual skin contact does not provide the usual transmission route.

30. Prenatal tests should not be misused for discriminatory:

  1. Understanding chromosome abnormalities
  2. Assessment of certain clinical conditions
  3. Sex selection
  4. Detection of every genetic disorder
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Sex selection

Ethical use concerns diagnostic care rather than discriminatory selection.
