Reproductive Health

Reproductive Health

Key concepts

  • Reproductive health includes physical, emotional and social well-being related to reproduction. Accurate education and accessible care are central.
  • Contraceptive methods differ in mechanism. Barrier methods can also reduce transmission of many sexually transmitted infections.
  • In vitro fertilisation involves fertilisation outside the body followed by embryo transfer. Assisted methods are selected by qualified clinicians.

Connect the ideas

A condom acts as a physical barrier; a contraceptive pill works through hormonal mechanisms. They are not interchangeable in all effects.

Common mistake

These notes describe exam concepts, not individual treatment advice. No contraceptive method guarantees prevention of every infection.

1. Which method is a barrier contraceptive?

  1. Condom
  2. IVF
  3. Embryo transfer
  4. Ovulation
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Condom

Condoms create a physical barrier.

2. In IVF, fertilisation occurs:

  1. Outside the body
  2. Only in the uterus
  3. Inside the urinary bladder
  4. In the cervix only
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Outside the body

Gametes are brought together outside the body under laboratory conditions.

3. Which statement about contraceptive methods is correct?

  1. All prevent every STI
  2. They differ in mechanism
  3. IVF is contraception
  4. All are surgical
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. They differ in mechanism

Barrier, hormonal, intrauterine and surgical methods use different mechanisms.
