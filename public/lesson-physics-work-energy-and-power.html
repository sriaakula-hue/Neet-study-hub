Work, energy and power

Work, energy and power

Core idea

Work by a constant force is W = Fs cos θ. Kinetic energy is K = ½mv². The net work done on a body equals its change in kinetic energy.

Worked connection

A 2 kg body moving at 3 m/s has kinetic energy 9 J. A perpendicular force does zero work on the instantaneous displacement.

Watch out

Power is the rate of doing work. For a constant force, instantaneous power is the dot product F · v.

Concept walkthrough and worked checkpoints

Use these seven checkpoints to connect the core ideas, practise applications and identify assumptions. Try each before opening its solution.

1. A 5 N force at 60° to a 4 m displacement does work:

10 J

W = Fs cos θ = 5 × 4 × 0.5 = 10 J.

2. A 2 kg body rises 3 m with g = 10 m/s². Its gravitational potential-energy increase is:

60 J

Near Earth’s surface, ΔU = mgh = 2 × 10 × 3 = 60 J.

3. A machine does 120 J of work in 4 s. Average power is:

30 W

Average power is work divided by elapsed time: 120/4 = 30 W.

4. A spring of k = 200 N/m is compressed 0.10 m. Stored energy is:

1 J

Elastic potential energy is ½kx² = ½ × 200 × 0.10² = 1 J.

5. Mechanical energy is conserved when work is done only by:

Conservative forces

With only conservative-force work, kinetic plus potential energy remains constant; nonconservative work can change that sum.

6. In an elastic collision of an isolated system, which is conserved?

Momentum and kinetic energy

Total linear momentum is conserved due to isolation, and total kinetic energy is conserved by the elastic-collision definition.

7. A body’s kinetic energy increases from 8 J to 20 J. Net work is:

12 J

The work–energy theorem gives Wnet = ΔK = 20 − 8 = 12 J.

Practice questions and explanations

Try each question before opening its answer.

1. A 2 kg body moving at 3 m/s has kinetic energy:

  1. 3 J
  2. 6 J
  3. 9 J
  4. 18 J
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. 9 J

K = ½mv² = ½ × 2 × 9 = 9 J.

2. A constant 10 N force acts along a displacement of 2 m. Its work is:

  1. 5 J
  2. 12 J
  3. 20 J
  4. 0 J
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. 20 J

W = Fs cos 0° = 10 × 2 = 20 J.

3. The SI unit of power is:

  1. Joule
  2. Newton
  3. Watt
  4. Pascal
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Watt

One watt is one joule per second.

4. A 5 N force at 60° to a 4 m displacement does work:

  1. 0 J
  2. 40 J
  3. 10 J
  4. 20 J
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. 10 J

W = Fs cos θ = 5 × 4 × 0.5 = 10 J.

8. Mechanical energy is conserved when work is done only by:

  1. Conservative forces
  2. Every friction force
  3. Any external force

Answer: A. Conservative forces

With only conservative-force work, kinetic plus potential energy remains constant; nonconservative work can change that sum.

Total linear momentum is conserved due to isolation, and total kinetic energy is conserved by the elastic-collision definition.
