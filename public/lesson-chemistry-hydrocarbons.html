Hydrocarbons

Hydrocarbons

Key concepts

  • Acyclic saturated alkanes have formula CnH₂n+₂. Acyclic alkenes with one double bond have CnH₂n; acyclic alkynes with one triple bond have CnH₂n−₂.
  • Addition reactions are characteristic of many unsaturated hydrocarbons. Alkanes can undergo radical substitution.
  • Benzene has a delocalised aromatic pi system and commonly undergoes electrophilic substitution.

Worked connection

Acyclic C₃H₈ matches an alkane formula. C₃H₆ can be an alkene or a cycloalkane, so formula alone does not establish structure.

Watch out

The peroxide effect for addition of hydrogen halides to alkenes is characteristic of HBr, not a universal rule for HCl and HI.

Concept walkthrough and worked checkpoints

Use these seven checkpoints to connect the core ideas, practise applications and identify assumptions. Try each before opening its solution.

1. Ethene contains how many pi bonds?

One

Its C=C double bond has one sigma bond and one pi bond.

2. Ethyne contains how many pi bonds between its carbons?

Two

The triple bond consists of one sigma and two pi bonds.

3. Hydrogenation of an alkene to an alkane is an:

Addition reaction

Hydrogen adds across the double bond under suitable catalytic conditions.

4. Acyclic compounds with one C≡C bond have general formula:

CnH₂n−₂

Relative to an alkane, one triple bond removes four hydrogen atoms.

5. In ordinary Markovnikov HBr addition to propene without peroxides, the major product is:

2-bromopropane

The usual ionic addition places Br on the more substituted carbon in this example.

6. Ethane’s carbon–carbon bond is:

A sigma bond

A single C–C bond is a sigma bond.

7. The simplest six-carbon aromatic ring is:

Benzene

Benzene has a cyclic conjugated six-pi-electron system; the other options are not equivalent aromatic structures.

  1. C₃H₄
  2. C₃H₆
  3. C₃H₈
  4. C₃H₁₀
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. C₃H₈

CnH₂n+₂ gives C₃H₈.

2. Benzene commonly undergoes:

  1. Electrophilic substitution
  2. Only nucleophilic addition
  3. Only polymerisation
  4. No reactions

  1. HCl
  2. HBr

Answer: B. HBr

The radical-chain route is effective for HBr under suitable conditions.

  1. One

Answer: A. One

  1. Two

  4. Three

  1. Addition reaction
