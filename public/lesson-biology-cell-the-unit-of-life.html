Cell: The Unit of Life

Cell structure and organelles

The cell is the basic structural and functional unit of life. Prokaryotic cells lack a membrane-bound nucleus. Eukaryotic cells have a membrane-bound nucleus and other membrane-bound organelles.

Key functions

Ribosomes synthesize proteins. Mitochondria carry out much of aerobic cellular respiration and ATP production in eukaryotic cells. Chloroplasts carry out photosynthesis in plant and algal cells that contain them.

Watch out

Ribosomes are not membrane-bound and occur in both prokaryotic and eukaryotic cells. Mature mammalian red blood cells lack nuclei and mitochondria.

Concept walkthrough and worked checkpoints

Use these seven checkpoints to connect the core ideas, practise applications and identify assumptions. Try each before opening its solution.

1. The plasma membrane is described as selectively permeable because it:

Regulates movement of substances

The lipid bilayer and membrane proteins determine how different substances cross.

2. The Golgi apparatus commonly participates in:

Modification and packaging of cell products

Golgi cisternae process, sort and package products arriving from the endoplasmic reticulum.

3. Rough endoplasmic reticulum is associated with attached:

Ribosomes

Attached ribosomes synthesise proteins entering the secretory or membrane pathway.

4. Lysosomes characteristically contain:

Hydrolytic enzymes

They support intracellular digestion and recycling under their appropriate internal conditions.

5. A nucleolus is strongly associated with:

Ribosome-component production

The nucleolus supports rRNA synthesis and ribosomal-subunit assembly.

6. Prokaryotic chromosomal material is generally located in the:

Nucleoid region

Prokaryotes lack a membrane-bound nucleus; their DNA occupies a nucleoid region.

7. Mitochondria and chloroplasts possess:

Their own DNA and ribosomes

These organelles retain genetic and protein-synthesis machinery, although many required proteins are encoded in the nucleus.

Practice questions and explanations

Try each question before opening its answer.

1. Which structure synthesizes proteins?

  1. Lysosome
  2. Ribosome
  3. Vacuole
  4. Cell wall
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Ribosome

Ribosomes translate mRNA into polypeptides during protein synthesis.

2. Which is characteristic of a prokaryotic cell?

  1. No ribosomes
  2. No cell membrane
  3. No membrane-bound nucleus
  4. Always multicellular
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. No membrane-bound nucleus

Prokaryotes have genetic material without a membrane-bound nucleus, and they do have ribosomes.

3. Which organelle carries out much aerobic ATP production in eukaryotic cells?

  1. Mitochondrion
  2. Golgi apparatus
  3. Cell wall
  4. Nucleolus
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Mitochondrion

Mitochondria host the citric acid cycle and oxidative phosphorylation.

4. The plasma membrane is described as selectively permeable because it:

  1. Regulates movement of substances
  2. Allows everything equally
  3. Allows nothing ever
  4. Is made only of cellulose
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Regulates movement of substances

The lipid bilayer and membrane proteins determine how different substances cross.

5. The Golgi apparatus commonly participates in:

  1. Modification and packaging of cell products
  2. Only DNA replication
  3. Only ATP synthesis
  4. Only photosynthetic oxygen release
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Modification and packaging of cell products

Golgi cisternae process, sort and package products arriving from the endoplasmic reticulum.

6. Rough endoplasmic reticulum is associated with attached:

  1. Ribosomes
  2. Chloroplasts
  3. Whole nuclei
  4. Centrioles only
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Ribosomes

Attached ribosomes synthesise proteins entering the secretory or membrane pathway.

7. Lysosomes characteristically contain:

  1. Hydrolytic enzymes
  2. Only chlorophyll
  3. Only DNA polymerase
  4. Only glucose
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Hydrolytic enzymes

They support intracellular digestion and recycling under their appropriate internal conditions.

8. A nucleolus is strongly associated with:

  1. Ribosome-component production
  2. Only lipid storage
  3. Only ATP transport
  4. Only cell-wall synthesis
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Ribosome-component production

The nucleolus supports rRNA synthesis and ribosomal-subunit assembly.

9. Prokaryotic chromosomal material is generally located in the:

  1. Nucleoid region
  2. Membrane-bound nucleus
  3. Golgi lumen
  4. Chloroplast stroma
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Nucleoid region

Prokaryotes lack a membrane-bound nucleus; their DNA occupies a nucleoid region.

10. Mitochondria and chloroplasts possess:

  1. Their own DNA and ribosomes
  2. No membranes
  3. Only cellulose
  4. No genetic material
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Their own DNA and ribosomes

These organelles retain genetic and protein-synthesis machinery, although many required proteins are encoded in the nucleus.
