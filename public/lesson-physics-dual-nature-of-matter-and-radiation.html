Dual Nature of Matter and Radiation

Dual Nature of Matter and Radiation

Key concepts

  • Photon energy is hf. In the photoelectric effect, maximum electron kinetic energy is hf − φ when the threshold is exceeded.
  • Threshold frequency is φ/h. For a given metal, increasing frequency above threshold increases maximum kinetic energy.
  • The de Broglie wavelength is h/p. Matter-wave descriptions apply to particles, though macroscopic wavelengths can be extremely small.

Worked example

A 5 eV photon incident on a surface with work function 2 eV can produce a maximum photoelectron kinetic energy of 3 eV.

Watch out

Increasing intensity below threshold does not produce photoemission in the usual single-photon model.

Concept walkthrough and worked checkpoints

Use these seven checkpoints to connect the core ideas, practise applications and identify assumptions. Try each before opening its solution.

1. Maximum photoelectron kinetic energy above threshold increases with incident light:

Frequency

Einstein’s relation Kmax = hf − φ links maximum energy to frequency for a fixed surface.

2. At fixed above-threshold frequency, greater intensity generally increases:

Photoelectron emission rate

More photons per time can emit more electrons, assuming suitable collection and no saturation limitation.

3. Stopping potential Vs relates to Kmax through:

eVs = Kmax

The retarding electrical potential removes the maximum kinetic energy eVs.

4. A photoelectron has Kmax = 4 eV. Stopping potential magnitude is:

4 V

One electron crossing 4 V changes electrical energy by 4 eV.

5. Doubling particle momentum changes de Broglie wavelength by:

Halving it

λ = h/p, so wavelength is inversely proportional to momentum.

6. For a photon, energy in terms of wavelength is:

hc/λ

Combine E = hf and f = c/λ to obtain E = hc/λ.

7. Work function is the minimum energy needed to:

Remove an electron from the surface

It is the material-dependent threshold energy for electron escape from the surface.

Practice questions and explanations

Try each question before opening its answer.

1. A photon of energy 5 eV with work function 2 eV gives Kmax:

  1. 2 eV
  2. 3 eV
  3. 5 eV
  4. 7 eV
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. 3 eV

Kmax = 5 − 2 = 3 eV.

2. The de Broglie wavelength is:

  1. hp
  2. p/h
  3. h/p
  4. h + p
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. h/p

Wavelength equals Planck’s constant divided by momentum.

3. Below threshold frequency, increasing intensity in the usual single-photon model gives:

  1. Photoemission necessarily
  2. No photoemission
  3. Higher Kmax
  4. Zero work function
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. No photoemission

Each photon still lacks the required energy.

4. Maximum photoelectron kinetic energy above threshold increases with incident light:

  1. Frequency
  2. Intensity alone at fixed frequency
  3. Beam area alone
  4. Polarisation only
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Frequency

Einstein’s relation Kmax = hf − φ links maximum energy to frequency for a fixed surface.

5. At fixed above-threshold frequency, greater intensity generally increases:

  1. Photoelectron emission rate
  2. Work function
  3. Threshold frequency
  4. Maximum kinetic energy necessarily
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Photoelectron emission rate

More photons per time can emit more electrons, assuming suitable collection and no saturation limitation.

6. Stopping potential Vs relates to Kmax through:

  1. eVs = Kmax
  2. Vs = Kmax²
  3. e/Vs = Kmax
  4. Vs = hf always
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. eVs = Kmax

The retarding electrical potential removes the maximum kinetic energy eVs.
