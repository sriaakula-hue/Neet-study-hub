Microbes in Human Welfare

Microbes in Human Welfare

Key concepts

  • Microbes support food processing, industrial production, sewage treatment and agriculture.
  • Saccharomyces cerevisiae is used in fermentation. Lactic acid bacteria help convert milk into curd.
  • Methanogenic microorganisms contribute to biogas production. Biological sewage treatment reduces organic load through microbial activity.

Connect the ideas

In fermentation, yeast can convert sugars into ethanol and carbon dioxide. Different processes use different microorganisms.

Common mistake

Not all microbes are pathogens; beneficial uses depend on choosing suitable organisms and conditions.

Concept walkthrough and worked checkpoints

Use these seven checkpoints to connect the core ideas, practise applications and identify assumptions. Try each before opening its solution.

1. Biological sewage treatment commonly uses microbial:

Flocs and metabolic activity

Microbial aggregates degrade organic matter, reducing oxygen demand under suitable treatment conditions.

2. BOD is a measure related to oxygen required for:

Biological decomposition of organic matter

Higher biochemical oxygen demand commonly indicates a greater biodegradable organic load.

3. Biogas methanogens belong chiefly to:

Archaea

Methanogenesis is characteristic of certain archaeal microorganisms in anaerobic settings.

4. Rhizobium can benefit leguminous plants by:

Symbiotic nitrogen fixation

Root-nodule associations convert atmospheric nitrogen into forms usable within the symbiosis.

5. A biofertiliser uses organisms that improve:

Nutrient availability or uptake

Examples include nitrogen-fixing bacteria and beneficial mycorrhizal associations.

6. Mycorrhiza describes an association between fungi and plant:

Roots

The partnership can improve mineral and water uptake while fungi receive organic products.

7. Penicillin was originally associated with a:

Fungus

Penicillium fungi produce penicillin-related compounds; antibiotics are distinct from vaccines.

S. cerevisiae is widely used for fermentation.
