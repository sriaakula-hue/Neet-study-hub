Locomotion and Movement

Locomotion and Movement

Key concepts

  • A sarcomere is the contractile unit between adjacent Z lines in a striated muscle fibre.
  • In sliding-filament contraction, actin and myosin filaments slide rather than shorten. Calcium binds to troponin and exposes binding sites.
  • ATP is needed for cross-bridge cycling, including detachment of myosin from actin. Joints permit different ranges and types of movement.

Connect the ideas

During contraction, the I band and H zone narrow, while the A band remains essentially constant in length.

Common mistake

Muscle contraction does not shorten the individual thick and thin filaments.

Concept walkthrough and worked checkpoints

Use these seven checkpoints to connect the core ideas, practise applications and identify assumptions. Try each before opening its solution.

1. Thin muscle filaments are chiefly associated with:

Actin

Actin forms the main thin-filament framework, with associated regulatory proteins.

2. Thick muscle filaments are chiefly composed of:

Myosin

Myosin heads form cross-bridges with actin during contraction.

3. ATP binding to a myosin head helps it:

Detach from actin

ATP attachment reduces actin affinity; subsequent hydrolysis supports the cross-bridge cycle.

4. A tendon connects:

Muscle to bone

Tendons transmit muscle pull to skeletal attachment points.

5. A ligament typically connects:

Bone to bone

Ligaments support joints by connecting bones and limiting excessive movement.

6. A ball-and-socket joint is found at the human:

Shoulder

The shoulder permits movement in multiple planes through a ball-and-socket arrangement.

7. The H zone mainly contains:

Thick filaments without thin-filament overlap

It is the central thick-filament region lacking actin overlap in the stated sarcomere arrangement.

  1. A bands
  2. Z lines
  3. H zones
  4. Nuclei

Answer: B. Z lines

Z lines delimit the sarcomere.

2. During skeletal muscle contraction, which band remains approximately constant?

  1. I band
  2. A band
  3. H zone
  4. All shrink equally
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. A band

The A band corresponds to thick-filament length, which stays constant.
