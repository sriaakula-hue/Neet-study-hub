Animal Kingdom

Animal Kingdom

Key concepts

  • Animal classification uses symmetry, germ layers, body cavity, segmentation and the presence of a notochord.
  • Cnidarians possess cnidocytes. Arthropods have jointed appendages. Echinoderms have a water vascular system.
  • Chordates possess a notochord at least during development, along with a dorsal hollow nerve cord and other defining traits.

Connect the ideas

An earthworm is an annelid, a cockroach is an arthropod and a starfish is an echinoderm.

Common mistake

Adult echinoderms generally have radial symmetry, while their larvae are bilaterally symmetrical.

Concept walkthrough and worked checkpoints

Use these seven checkpoints to connect the core ideas, practise applications and identify assumptions. Try each before opening its solution.

1. Poriferans characteristically have a:

Canal system

Water enters pores and passes through a canal system, supporting feeding, respiration and waste removal.

2. Flatworms are generally:

Acoelomate

Platyhelminthes lack a body cavity between the body wall and digestive tract.

3. Roundworms are typically:

Pseudocoelomate

Their body cavity is not completely lined by mesoderm, so it is a pseudocoelom.

4. Annelids show characteristic:

Metameric segmentation

Repeated body segments with corresponding organisation are characteristic of annelids.

5. Adult echinoderms and their larvae commonly differ because adults are radial while larvae are:

Bilateral

This life-stage contrast is a frequent classification checkpoint.

6. A dorsal hollow nerve cord is a characteristic chordate feature, unlike the typical:

Ventral solid nerve cord of many nonchordates

Chordate neural anatomy is distinguished by dorsal position and a hollow tubular cord.

7. A mantle is characteristically associated with:

Mollusca

The molluscan mantle covers the visceral mass and can secrete the shell.

Practice questions and explanations

1. Jointed appendages are characteristic of:

  1. Arthropoda
  2. Porifera
  3. Cnidaria
  4. Platyhelminthes
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Arthropoda

Arthropods possess jointed appendages and an exoskeleton.

2. A water vascular system is characteristic of:

  1. Annelida
  2. Mollusca
  3. Echinodermata
  4. Nematoda
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Echinodermata

Echinoderms use a water vascular system with tube feet.

3. Cnidocytes are characteristic of:

  1. Chordata
  2. Cnidaria
  3. Arthropoda
  4. Annelida
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Cnidaria

Cnidocytes are the specialised stinging cells of cnidarians.

4. Poriferans characteristically have a:

  1. Canal system
  2. Vertebral column
  3. Jointed exoskeleton
  4. True coelom with organs
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Canal system

Water enters pores and passes through a canal system, supporting feeding, respiration and waste removal.

5. Flatworms are generally:

  1. Acoelomate
  2. Pseudocoelomate
  3. True coelomate
  4. Radially symmetric only
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Acoelomate

Platyhelminthes lack a body cavity between the body wall and digestive tract.

6. Roundworms are typically:

  1. Pseudocoelomate
  2. Acoelomate
  3. Vertebrates
  4. Diploblastic cnidarians
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Pseudocoelomate

Their body cavity is not completely lined by mesoderm, so it is a pseudocoelom.

7. Annelids show characteristic:

  1. Metameric segmentation
  2. Jointed appendages
  3. Notochord in all adults
  4. Water vascular systems
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Metameric segmentation

Repeated body segments with corresponding organisation are characteristic of annelids.

8. Adult echinoderms and their larvae commonly differ because adults are radial while larvae are:

  1. Bilateral
  2. Always asymmetrical
  3. Without cells
  4. Only segmented

Answer: A. Bilateral

This life-stage contrast is a frequent classification checkpoint.

Answer: A. Ventral solid nerve cord of many nonchordates

Chordate neural anatomy is distinguished by dorsal position and a hollow tubular cord.

  1. Mollusca
