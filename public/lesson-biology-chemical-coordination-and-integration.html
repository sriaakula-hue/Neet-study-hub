Chemical Coordination and Integration

Chemical Coordination and Integration

Key concepts

  • Endocrine glands release hormones into the circulation. Hormones act on target cells with appropriate receptors.
  • Insulin generally lowers blood glucose by promoting uptake and storage; glucagon promotes processes that raise it.
  • Thyroid hormones require iodine. Negative feedback regulates many endocrine axes; hormone concentration alone does not define all effects.

Connect the ideas

Rising thyroid hormone levels can reduce stimulation from the hypothalamus and pituitary through negative feedback.

Common mistake

Not every cell responds to every hormone. Target-cell receptors and downstream signalling determine responsiveness.
