Ray optics: lenses

Ray optics: lenses

Core idea

For a thin lens with the Cartesian sign convention, 1/f = 1/v − 1/u. Lens power P = 1/f when f is measured in metres. A converging lens has positive power.

Worked connection

A converging lens with focal length +0.50 m has power +2 D. A diverging lens with focal length −0.25 m has power −4 D.

Watch out

Use a consistent sign convention. A real object placed beyond 2f of a converging lens forms a real, inverted, diminished image between f and 2f.

Wave optics connection

For ideal Young double-slit interference, fringe width is β = λD/d when D is much larger than slit separation d. Coherent sources have a stable phase difference. Polarisation of light demonstrates its transverse character.

Concept walkthrough and worked checkpoints

Use these seven checkpoints to connect the core ideas, practise applications and identify assumptions. Try each before opening its solution.

1. A thin converging lens has f = +0.20 m. Power is:

+5 D

Use metres in P = 1/f: 1/0.20 = +5 dioptres.

2. A ray through the optical centre of an ideal thin lens is approximately:

Undeviated

In the thin-lens approximation, this construction ray continues approximately straight through.

3. Total internal reflection requires light travelling from:

Higher to lower refractive index, above the critical angle

A critical angle exists for higher-to-lower index transmission; incidence beyond it gives total internal reflection.

4. For λ = 500 nm, D = 2 m and slit separation d = 1 mm, Young fringe width is:

1 mm

β = λD/d = 500 × 10⁻⁹ × 2 / 10⁻³ = 10⁻³ m = 1 mm.

5. Sustained interference requires sources with:

Stable relative phase

Coherence means a stable phase relation; equal intensity alone is insufficient.

6. A convex lens with a real object between the lens and focus forms an image that is:

Virtual, upright and enlarged

The emerging rays diverge; their backward extensions meet on the object side, forming an enlarged virtual image.

7. Light polarisation demonstrates that light waves are:

Transverse

Polarisation restricts the orientation of transverse oscillations and therefore reveals transverse character.

Practice questions and explanations

Try each question before opening its answer.

1. A converging lens has focal length +0.50 m. Its power is:

  1. +0.5 D
  2. +2 D
  3. −2 D
  4. +50 D
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. +2 D

P = 1/f = 1/0.50 = +2 dioptres.

2. For a real object beyond 2f of a converging lens, the image is:

  1. Real, inverted and diminished
  2. Virtual and enlarged
  3. Real and upright
  4. At infinity
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Real, inverted and diminished

It is formed between f and 2f and is real, inverted and smaller.

3. A diverging lens with f = −0.25 m has power:

  1. +4 D
  2. −0.25 D
  3. −4 D
  4. +0.25 D
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. −4 D

P = 1/(−0.25) = −4 D.

4. A thin converging lens has f = +0.20 m. Power is:

  1. +20 D
  2. +5 D
  3. +0.2 D
  4. −5 D
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. +5 D

Use metres in P = 1/f: 1/0.20 = +5 dioptres.

5. A ray through the optical centre of an ideal thin lens is approximately:

  1. Undeviated
  2. Always reflected back
  3. Absorbed
  4. Bent by exactly 90°
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Undeviated

In the thin-lens approximation, this construction ray continues approximately straight through.

6. Total internal reflection requires light travelling from:

  1. Higher to lower refractive index, above the critical angle
  2. Lower to higher index at any angle
  3. Vacuum to glass at any angle
  4. Any medium at normal incidence
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Higher to lower refractive index, above the critical angle

A critical angle exists for higher-to-lower index transmission; incidence beyond it gives total internal reflection.

7. For λ = 500 nm, D = 2 m and slit separation d = 1 mm, Young fringe width is:

  1. 100 mm
  2. 1 mm
  3. 0.1 mm
  4. 10 mm
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. 1 mm

β = λD/d = 500 × 10⁻⁹ × 2 / 10⁻³ = 10⁻³ m = 1 mm.

8. Sustained interference requires sources with:

  1. Stable relative phase
  2. Only equal brightness
  3. Only different frequencies
  4. No wave character
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Stable relative phase

Coherence means a stable phase relation; equal intensity alone is insufficient.

9. A convex lens with a real object between the lens and focus forms an image that is:

  1. Virtual, upright and enlarged
  2. Real and diminished
  3. Real and inverted at all distances
  4. At the focus always
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Virtual, upright and enlarged

The emerging rays diverge; their backward extensions meet on the object side, forming an enlarged virtual image.

10. Light polarisation demonstrates that light waves are:

  1. Transverse
  2. Purely longitudinal
  3. Sound waves
  4. Without electric fields
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Transverse

Polarisation restricts the orientation of transverse oscillations and therefore reveals transverse character.
