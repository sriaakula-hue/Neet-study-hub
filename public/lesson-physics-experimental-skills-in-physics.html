Experimental Skills in Physics

Experimental Skills in Physics

Key concepts

  • Instrument readings require attention to least count, zero error and the relevant measurement convention.
  • For an ideal small-angle simple pendulum, T² = (4π²/g)L. The slope of T² plotted against L is 4π²/g.
  • Ohmic resistance can be obtained as the slope of V plotted against I under fixed physical conditions. Choose graph axes before interpreting slopes.

Worked example

If a V-against-I graph rises 6 V for a 2 A current increase, its slope and resistance are 3 Ω.

Watch out

Axis reversal changes the meaning of slope. An I-against-V slope is conductance, not resistance. Practical experiments require appropriate supervision.

Concept walkthrough and worked checkpoints

Use these seven checkpoints to connect the core ideas, practise applications and identify assumptions. Try each before opening its solution.

1. A graph of I against V for an ohmic resistor has slope:

Conductance

Slope ΔI/ΔV = 1/R, which is conductance; reversing axes changes interpretation.

2. Repeated measurements mainly help estimate:

Random variation

Repeats reveal scatter and improve estimates, but a fixed calibration error can persist in every reading.

3. An instrument with a smaller least count generally offers:

Finer reading resolution

Smaller readable increments improve resolution, but accuracy still depends on calibration and measurement method.

4. Observed length is 5.12 cm with zero error +0.02 cm. Corrected length is:

5.10 cm

Corrected reading = observed − signed zero error = 5.12 − 0.02 = 5.10 cm.

5. Parallax error is reduced by viewing a scale:

With the eye aligned appropriately perpendicular to the scale

Proper eye alignment prevents apparent displacement between marker and scale due to viewing angle.

6. For a metre bridge balanced at length l cm from one end, the ideal resistance ratio is:

l/(100 − l)

With uniform wire and resistances assigned consistently to the two segments, the balance ratio equals their length ratio.

7. For a simple pendulum experiment, timing many oscillations instead of one chiefly reduces relative:

Timing reaction error

A roughly fixed start/stop reaction uncertainty becomes a smaller fraction of the longer measured interval.

Practice questions and explanations

Try each question before opening its answer.

1. For an ohmic V-against-I graph, slope gives:

  1. Resistance
  2. Conductance
  3. Power always
  4. Charge
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Resistance

Slope ΔV/ΔI is resistance.

2. If ΔV = 6 V and ΔI = 2 A, the resistance is:

  1. 0.33 Ω
  2. 3 Ω
  3. 8 Ω
  4. 12 Ω
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. 3 Ω

R = ΔV/ΔI = 6/2 = 3 Ω.

3. A pendulum T²-against-L slope is:

  1. g/4π²
  2. 4π²/g
  3. 2πg
  4. Always g
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. 4π²/g

Squaring T = 2π√(L/g) gives T² = (4π²/g)L.

4. A graph of I against V for an ohmic resistor has slope:

  1. Conductance
  2. Resistance
  3. Power
  4. Charge
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Conductance

Slope ΔI/ΔV = 1/R, which is conductance; reversing axes changes interpretation.

5. Repeated measurements mainly help estimate:

  1. Random variation
  2. All systematic errors automatically
  3. Exactly zero uncertainty
  4. Only the unit name
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Random variation

Repeats reveal scatter and improve estimates, but a fixed calibration error can persist in every reading.

6. An instrument with a smaller least count generally offers:

  1. Finer reading resolution
  2. Guaranteed zero error
  3. No need for calibration
  4. A different physical dimension
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Finer reading resolution

Smaller readable increments improve resolution, but accuracy still depends on calibration and measurement method.

7. Observed length is 5.12 cm with zero error +0.02 cm. Corrected length is:

  1. 5.14 cm
  2. 5.12 cm
  3. 5.00 cm
  4. 5.10 cm
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. 5.10 cm

Corrected reading = observed − signed zero error = 5.12 − 0.02 = 5.10 cm.

8. Parallax error is reduced by viewing a scale:

  1. With the eye aligned appropriately perpendicular to the scale
  2. From arbitrary oblique angles
  3. Only in darkness
  4. From farther away at any angle
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. With the eye aligned appropriately perpendicular to the scale

Proper eye alignment prevents apparent displacement between marker and scale due to viewing angle.

9. For a metre bridge balanced at length l cm from one end, the ideal resistance ratio is:

  1. l/(100 − l)
  2. 100/l always
  3. l/100 always
  4. (100 − l)/100
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. l/(100 − l)

With uniform wire and resistances assigned consistently to the two segments, the balance ratio equals their length ratio.

10. For a simple pendulum experiment, timing many oscillations instead of one chiefly reduces relative:

  1. Timing reaction error
  2. Gravitational force
  3. String length
  4. Mass
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Timing reaction error

A roughly fixed start/stop reaction uncertainty becomes a smaller fraction of the longer measured interval.

11. A vernier caliper has 10 vernier divisions equal to 9 main-scale divisions. One main-scale division is 0.2 mm. Least count is:

  1. 0.03 mm
  2. 0.04 mm
  3. 0.02 mm
  4. 0.01 mm
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. 0.02 mm

Least count=1 MSD−1 VSD; VSD=0.9 MSD, so LC=0.1 MSD. Answer: 0.02 mm.

12. A screw gauge has pitch 0.2 mm and 100 circular divisions. Least count is:

  1. 0.004 mm
  2. 0.002 mm
  3. 0.001 mm
  4. 0.003 mm
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. 0.002 mm

Least count=pitch/number of circular divisions. Answer: 0.002 mm.

13. A metre bridge balances at 40 cm from the unknown-resistance end. The other gap has 6 Ω. Unknown resistance is:

  1. 4 Ω
  2. 2 Ω
  3. 6 Ω
  4. 8 Ω
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. 4 Ω

At balance, R/S=ℓ/(100−ℓ)=40/60=2/3. Answer: 4 Ω.

14. A potentiometer has potential gradient 0.02 V/cm. A cell balances at 20 cm. Its emf is:

  1. 0.2 V
  2. 0.6 V
  3. 0.8 V
  4. 0.4 V
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. 0.4 V

At null balance, cell emf equals potential gradient multiplied by balance length. Answer: 0.4 V.

15. An ammeter reads 0.5 A and a voltmeter reads 2 V across an ideal ohmic test resistor. Resistance is:

  1. 6 Ω
  2. 8 Ω
  3. 4 Ω
  4. 2 Ω
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. 4 Ω

Using ideal meters, R=V/I=k/0.5=2k Ω. Answer: 4 Ω.
