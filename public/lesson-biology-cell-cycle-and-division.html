Cell cycle and division

Cell cycle and division

Core idea

The cell cycle includes interphase and the division phase. DNA replication occurs in S phase. Mitosis usually preserves chromosome number. Meiosis reduces chromosome number and contributes to genetic variation.

Worked connection

A diploid cell with 2n = 8 produces meiotic products with n = 4, under normal segregation.

Watch out

Crossing over occurs during pachytene of prophase I. Homologous chromosomes separate in anaphase I, while sister chromatids separate in anaphase II.

Practice questions and explanations

Try each question before opening its answer.

1. DNA replication occurs during:

  1. G₁ phase
  2. S phase
  3. G₂ phase only
  4. Anaphase
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. S phase

The S (synthesis) phase is when DNA is replicated.

2. A diploid cell has 2n = 8. Its normal meiotic products have:

  1. 2 chromosomes
  2. 4 chromosomes
  3. 8 chromosomes
  4. 16 chromosomes
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. 4 chromosomes

Meiosis halves the chromosome number to n = 4.

3. Homologous chromosomes separate during:

  1. Anaphase I
  2. Prophase I
  3. Metaphase II
  4. Interphase
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Anaphase I

Anaphase I separates homologous chromosomes; anaphase II separates sister chromatids.

4. The G₁ phase is commonly associated with growth before:

  1. DNA synthesis
  2. Meiosis II only
  3. Cytokinesis only
  4. Cell-wall destruction
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. DNA synthesis

G₁ precedes the S phase of interphase, when DNA replication occurs.

5. Replication in S phase changes DNA amount in a typical diploid cell but not immediately its:

  1. Chromosome number counted by centromeres
  2. DNA content
  3. Chromatid number
  4. Replication status
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Chromosome number counted by centromeres

Each chromosome gains a sister chromatid while remaining one chromosome until chromatids separate.

6. Chromosomes align at the equatorial region during mitotic:

  1. Metaphase
  2. Prophase only
  3. Telophase only
  4. G₁
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Metaphase

Metaphase alignment prepares chromosomes for orderly sister-chromatid separation.

7. Sister chromatids separate in mitotic:

  1. Anaphase
  2. Prophase
  3. Metaphase
  4. S phase
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Anaphase

Anaphase separates sister chromatids, which then count as daughter chromosomes.

8. Crossing over characteristically occurs during meiotic:

  1. Pachytene of prophase I
  2. Anaphase II
  3. Telophase II
  4. G₁ only
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Pachytene of prophase I

Paired homologous chromosomes exchange segments between nonsister chromatids during pachytene.

9. Synapsis is pairing of:

  1. Homologous chromosomes
  2. Two unrelated nuclei
  3. Only ribosomes
  4. Only daughter cells
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Homologous chromosomes

Homologues pair during prophase I, forming bivalents.

10. Meiosis includes how many DNA-replication rounds before its two divisions?

  1. One
  2. Two
  3. Four
  4. Zero normally
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. One

A single premeiotic S phase is followed by meiosis I and II, without another DNA-replication round between them.

11. A typical diploid cell's DNA amount doubles during:

  1. Telophase alone
  2. S phase
  3. G₁ phase exclusively
  4. Cytokinesis
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. S phase

DNA replication occurs in the synthesis phase before division.

12. After S phase, a typical diploid cell has chromosome number:

  1. 2n
  2. 4n necessarily
  3. n
  4. Zero
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. 2n

DNA doubles, but centromere-based chromosome counting remains diploid.

13. The G₂ phase chiefly prepares the cell for:

  1. Fertilisation immediately
  2. Only photosynthesis
  3. Only meiosis II in every cell
  4. Mitosis
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Mitosis

Growth and synthesis support the upcoming division machinery.

14. Chromosomes become visibly condensed during mitotic:

  1. Cytokinesis only
  2. G₀ only
  3. Prophase
  4. G₁ exclusively
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Prophase

Chromatin compaction produces recognisable mitotic chromosomes.

15. Chromosomes align at the equatorial plate during:

  1. Interphase
  2. Metaphase
  3. Prophase only
  4. Telophase
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Metaphase

Spindle attachment positions chromosomes at the cell's equator.

16. Sister chromatids separate during mitotic:

  1. Anaphase
  2. Metaphase
  3. Prophase
  4. G₁
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Anaphase

Centromere separation permits chromatids to move to opposite poles.

17. Nuclear envelopes commonly re-form during:

  1. Metaphase
  2. Anaphase onset
  3. S phase
  4. Telophase
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Telophase

Chromosome decondensation accompanies nuclear-envelope reassembly.

18. Cytokinesis refers to division of the:

  1. Centromere only
  2. Nucleolus only
  3. Cytoplasm
  4. DNA base only
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Cytoplasm

It separates the daughter-cell cytoplasmic compartments.

19. Animal-cell cytokinesis commonly uses a:

  1. Thylakoid stack
  2. Cleavage furrow
  3. Cell plate
  4. New woody wall
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Cleavage furrow

Contractile machinery constricts the plasma membrane.

20. Plant-cell cytokinesis commonly involves formation of a:

  1. Cell plate
  2. Cleavage furrow only
  3. Synaptonemal complex
  4. Centriolar ring
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Cell plate

Vesicles build a plate that develops into separating cell walls.

21. The division that reduces chromosome-set number is:

  1. Mitosis
  2. Meiosis II alone
  3. Binary fission in every species
  4. Meiosis I
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Meiosis I

Homologous chromosomes separate during the reductional division.

22. Homologous chromosomes pair during meiotic:

  1. Telophase II
  2. Mitosis anaphase
  3. Prophase I
  4. Prophase II exclusively
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Prophase I

Synapsis and bivalent formation occur in the first meiotic prophase.

23. Synapsis is most closely associated with:

  1. Telophase II
  2. Zygotene
  3. Leptotene only
  4. Diakinesis only
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Zygotene

Homologues pair as the synaptonemal complex forms.

24. Crossing over is typically associated with:

  1. Pachytene
  2. Zygotene onset only
  3. G₁
  4. Metaphase II
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Pachytene

Recombination occurs between nonsister chromatids of paired homologues.

25. Chiasmata become conspicuous in:

  1. G₂
  2. Anaphase II
  3. Cytokinesis
  4. Diplotene
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Diplotene

Homologues begin separating except at crossover-associated contacts.

26. A bivalent contains how many chromatids after replication?

  1. 2
  2. 8
  3. 4
  4. 1
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. 4

Two homologous chromosomes each have two sister chromatids.

27. In anaphase I, separating structures are primarily:

  1. Only nucleoli
  2. Homologous chromosomes
  3. Sister chromatids with split centromeres
  4. Only ribosomes
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Homologous chromosomes

Centromeres typically remain intact in the first meiotic separation.

28. In anaphase II, separating structures are primarily:

  1. Sister chromatids
  2. Intact homologous pairs
  3. Only cell walls
  4. Only spindle proteins
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Sister chromatids

Centromere separation resembles mitotic chromatid separation.

29. The interval between meiotic divisions ordinarily lacks a full:

  1. Chromosome presence
  2. Cytoplasm
  3. Membrane
  4. DNA replication phase
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. DNA replication phase

Interkinesis does not normally duplicate DNA again.

30. A typical completed meiosis of one diploid precursor yields:

  1. Eight tetraploid products
  2. One diploid product
  3. Four haploid products
  4. Two diploid products
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Four haploid products

Two successive divisions follow one DNA replication round.
